### System Prototyping and Interface Design

The user interface of the CCIS-CodeHub platform was designed with a focus on modern aesthetic principles, usability, and cognitive load reduction, employing a cohesive dark mode theme to reduce eye strain during prolonged academic use. 

#### 1. Landing Page (System Entry and Onboarding)
The Landing Page serves as the primary entry point for prospective and returning students of the College of Computing and Information Sciences (CCIS). The interface adopts a minimalist, high-contrast dark theme utilizing deep blues and purples to convey a modern, technology-focused academic environment. Central to the design is a clear, action-oriented typography ("Learn. Build. Grow.") that aligns perfectly with the system's core objectives: structured coursework, hands-on project management, and continuous development via AI mentoring. Below the primary Call-to-Action (CTA) buttons, dynamic visual elements and a statistical counter (e.g., "7+ Students", "5+ Courses") establish immediate credibility and social proof, encouraging user registration and platform adoption.

#### 2. Learning Center Module
The Learning Center interface is architected to prioritize content discoverability and active student engagement. The layout utilizes a card-based grid system to cleanly separate active enrollments from available courses. Key performance indicators, such as the total number of courses and active enrollments, are prominently displayed at the top to provide students with an immediate overview of their academic standing. Each course card (e.g., "Hosting a Website on AWS EC2", "Comprehensive Data Structures") encapsulates essential metadata, including course duration, module count, and difficulty level (e.g., 'beginner', 'advanced'). Furthermore, the integration of a dynamic progress bar for enrolled courses (e.g., "33% Your Progress") applies psychological design principles to motivate continuous learning and task completion among students.

#### 3. Projects Hub (Agile Task Management)
The Projects Hub module translates industry-standard Agile methodologies into an accessible academic environment. The dashboard is highly analytical, featuring summary metrics (Total Projects, Active Projects, Total Tasks) that grant users a macro-level view of their collaborative efforts. The core functional area utilizes an interactive Kanban board interface, categorizing tasks into standardized workflow states: "To Do", "In Progress", "Review", and "Done". By visualizing the workflow, the interface mitigates the frequent communication breakdowns common in student group work. The inclusion of priority badges (e.g., "medium"), assignees, and due dates on individual task cards ensures strict accountability and aids in teaching students fundamental software engineering project management practices.

#### 4. AI Mentor Integration (Asynchronous Support)
The AI Mentor interface demonstrates a seamless integration of artificial intelligence within the educational workflow. Rather than redirecting the student to an external application, the AI Mentor operates as a persistent, non-intrusive floating widget accessible directly from the Learning Center. The chat interface is designed for conversational interaction, offering predefined, structured query options (e.g., "Navigating the platform", "Technical coding questions") alongside a free-text input field. This dual-input approach lowers the barrier to entry for users unfamiliar with prompt engineering. By providing immediate, context-aware assistance, the interface effectively reduces the dependency on synchronous instructor availability, thereby mitigating student isolated learning roadblocks.

#### 5. Admin Dashboard (System Analytics and Moderation)
The Admin Dashboard operates as the central command center for system-wide analytics and user management, adhering to strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) principles. Designed for high-level administrative oversight, the interface prioritizes data visualization over dense tabular formats. It employs a grid of dynamic, interactive charts—such as donut charts for "Users by Role" (e.g., Student 57%, Instructor 29%, Admin 14%) and "Users by Program" alongside bar graphs for "Students by Year Level." This graphical approach to data presentation facilitates rapid cognitive processing, allowing administrators to instantly evaluate system adoption metrics, monitor active projects, and manage content moderation workflows without navigating through complex sub-menus. The sidebar navigation reinforces clear systemic boundaries between Learning Administration, Project Administration, and User Management.

#### 6. Instructor Dashboard (Course Administration)
The Instructor Dashboard is tailored to facilitate the efficient creation and management of educational curricula with minimal friction. The interface presents a clear hierarchy of primary actions at the top of the view: "Create Path," "Create Module," and "AI Content" generation, directly empowering educators to build and scale their instructional materials. Below the action array, metric cards provide real-time status updates on the instructor’s portfolio, quantifying active "Career Paths," "Modules," "Quizzes," and overall student "Enrollments." This design eliminates the administrative bottleneck often found in legacy Learning Management Systems (LMS) by providing educators with a unified, high-level overview of their generated content and immediate pathways to expand upon it, ultimately enriching the platform's learning ecosystem.

#### 7. Student Dashboard (Personalized Learning Pathways)
The Student Dashboard is engineered to provide a personalized, distraction-free environment focused entirely on the individual learner's progression. The interface categorizes the student's academic journey through a tabbed system for "All," "In Progress," and "Completed" courses, promoting a strong sense of organization. Each enrolled course (e.g., "Fundamentals of SQL", "Hosting a Website on AWS EC2") is visualized as a distinct list item featuring a prominent circular progression indicator. The presence of clear "100%" completion badges and "Done" status markers leverages positive reinforcement to encourage continuous engagement. The localized navigation menu on the left ensures that while the student is focused on their specific modules, access to broader platform features like Certificates, Projects, and the Community remains highly accessible yet unobtrusive.
